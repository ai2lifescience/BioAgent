Use case: scientific-educational.
Create a BRAND NEW, beautifully art-directed scientific publication architecture figure for a bioinformatics pipeline agent. Generate the image from scratch. No reference images. The intended result is a polished journal graphical abstract with professional scientific illustrations, not a generic slide or a wall of boxes. Landscape approximately 16:9, high resolution, white background, generous whitespace, crisp legible typography, restrained flat colors and delicate vector-like scientific linework.

CORE MESSAGE, prominent exact equation, with no separate title above it:
"Pipeline Agent = LLM + Harness + Tools"

Composition: a coherent, balanced left-to-right architecture with THREE clearly distinguished main regions, LLM on left, Harness in center, Tools on right. The Harness is the coordination hub, with meaningful bidirectional arrows between LLM and Harness, and between Harness and Tools. Above the hub, a small researcher illustration provides human interaction and oversight. Below, a narrow shared workspace and results area makes the real-file workflow tangible. Do not put numbers on the components. Use elegant scientific illustrations as the primary communication, only short labels; no bullet paragraphs. Architectural relationships must be simple and unambiguous. All labels sit clearly outside arrows; no overlapping lines or dangling connections.

Visual style: exceptional editorial design for a computational biology paper, guided by the soft palette and typography of `good color style.png`. Fine, consistent strokes; dark navy text; powder blue for LLM, pale lavender for Harness, muted mint for Tools, and restrained warm accents only where needed. Use low-saturation fills and medium-blue scientific illustrations; avoid bright cyan, neon teal, saturated violet, harsh orange, glossy gradients and heavy shadows. Use one consistent humanist sans-serif family with comfortable spacing, semibold headings, medium section labels, regular explanatory text, deep slate/navy primary text and subdued slate blue-gray secondary text. Keep the main equation accents muted. Restrained pale tinted regions, visually open rather than thick closed boxes. Draw purposeful scientific icons with detail and craftsmanship: a symmetric, evenly spaced neural-network motif and branching plan for LLM; an orchestration/feedback motif for Harness; DNA and protein ribbon, workflow DAG with compute nodes, indexed literature, and files/chart for Tools. These are conceptual illustrations, not data plots and not decorations. White space, hierarchy and balance matter more than volume of labels. Avoid robot heads, cartoon brains, glowing sci-fi, dark backgrounds, dashboards, stock clip art, oversized title banners, excessive shadows, 3D isometric blocks, pseudo-code, microscopic text, logos, watermarks.
