Limitations
Bacterial only — this pipeline uses Prokka/Bakta which are designed for bacterial genomes. For viral or eukaryotic FASTA annotation, consider viral_genome_mutation_analysis (variant-focused) or use the general sequence tools (sequence_find_orfs, sequence_translate) directly.
Assembled contigs required — raw reads must first be assembled; for that, use metagenomic_de_novo_assembly (with the caveat that it prefers FASTQ input).
Annotation quality depends on the selected tool (prokka vs bakta) and database versions.
No taxonomy or abundance — this is purely structural/functional annotation, not metagenomic profiling.

---

Other FASTA-compatible alternatives worth noting
Bacterial functional annotation — if you need functional term assignments (e.g. KEGG/COG) rather than full structural gene annotation, this is the complementary option.
RNA secondary-structure prediction — if your FASTA contains RNA or DNA sequences for structure prediction rather than gene annotation.
Antimicrobial resistance detection / Virulence-factor detection — if your annotation goal is specifically to find resistance or virulence genes in the FASTA.
